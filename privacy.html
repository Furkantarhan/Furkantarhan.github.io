Summary: None of these apps ask you to create an account or collect personal information directly from you. Your entries, saved numbers, scores, settings and game progress stay on your device. Internet access is used only to load ads, diagnostics and, in FL Winning Numbers, publicly published draw results.

1. Data we collect

None directly. These apps never ask for your name, email address, phone number, payment details or any other personally identifying information. Your records, saved ticket numbers, scores, settings and progress are stored locally in your device storage. We cannot access that data.

2. Where your data lives

  • Your app data stays on your device and is removed when you clear app storage or uninstall the app.
  • Budget Book can export your records to a CSV file on demand. Where that file goes afterwards is entirely under your control.
  • If Android Backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up local app data to your Google account. That backup is governed by Google’s terms; we have no access to it.

3. Ads, analytics and third-party SDKs

These apps are free to use and display ads through Google AdMob (and, in Unity-based games, Unity Engine / Unity Services runtime components). To serve, cap and measure ads and monitor stability, third-party SDKs may automatically collect and share:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (city or country level)
  • Device model, operating system version, language and screen metrics
  • App interactions, ad impressions and clicks
  • Crash logs, diagnostics and app performance metrics

All data transmitted by these SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and processed under the respective provider’s privacy policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, AdMob data disclosures, and Unity Privacy Policy. We do not operate our own backend servers or sell user data.

4. Your ad choices

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are presented with a consent prompt on first launch via Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You may accept or decline personalised ads; declining does not disable app functionality. Where supported, you can update your preference under in-app Settings, and you can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time under Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

5. Permissions

Depending on the title, these apps request internet and network-state access, advertising ID permissions, vibration/haptic feedback, and optional local notification permissions (for draw reminders). None of our apps request access to your contacts, precise GPS location, microphone, camera, SMS messages or call logs.

6. Independent reference disclaimer (FL Winning Numbers)

FL Winning Numbers (com.simealapps.fllrapp) is an independent, unofficial informational reference tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to the Florida Lottery, any state lottery operator, or any government agency. You cannot purchase tickets, place bets, or claim prizes through the app.

7. Children

These applications are intended for general/adult audiences aged 18 and over and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights & data deletion

Because we do not create user accounts or store personal data on our own servers, there is no server-side profile for us to look up or delete. You can erase all local app data at any time from inside the app or by uninstalling it, and you can reset your Advertising ID in Android settings. For enquiries regarding data processed by Google AdMob, please refer to Google’s privacy controls.

9. Changes

If we update this policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

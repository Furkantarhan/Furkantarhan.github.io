Privacy Policy

Simeal Apps · Last updated 2 November 2026

This policy covers the following Android applications published by Simeal Apps:

  • Budget Book — com.simealapps.butcedefteri
  • Neon Block Blast — com.simealapps.neonblockblast
  • Strata Merge — com.simealgames.mergequest
Summary: None of these apps ask you to create an account or collect personal information. Everything you do in them stays on your device. The only time they use the internet is to load ads.

1. Data we collect

None. These apps never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other identifying information. Your entries, scores, settings and progress are stored only in your device storage. We cannot reach that data.

2. Where your data lives

  • Your data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Budget Book can export your records to a CSV file. Where that file goes afterwards is entirely under your control.
  • If Android Backup is enabled, the operating system may back up app data to your Google account. That backup is between you and Google; we have no access to it.

3. Ads and third parties

These apps are free and show ads through Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google may access:

  • Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address (city or country level)
  • Device model and operating system version
  • Ad impressions, clicks and other interaction data
  • Diagnostics, crash logs and performance data

This data is processed by Google under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and AdMob data disclosures. We do not collect, store or sell this data ourselves.

4. Your ad choice

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent screen on first launch, shown through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You may accept or refuse personalised ads; refusing disables no feature. You can change your choice later in the app under Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier from your device under Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Permissions

These apps request internet access and the advertising identifier permission, used only to load ads. They do not request access to your contacts, location services, camera, photos, SMS messages or call logs.

6. Children

These apps are intended for adults aged 18 and over and are not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children — we do not collect data from anyone.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is no access or deletion request to send us. You can delete your data from inside each app, or by uninstalling it. For requests about the advertising data in section 3, contact Google directly.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated.
